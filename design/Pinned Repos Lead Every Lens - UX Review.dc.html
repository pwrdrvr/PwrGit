0The ask, and what this round assumes

Operator, 2026-11-17. This board reverses the decision recorded in Repo Index Reorder, 2b (“PwrAgnt’s pin seam, in every lens: not recommended”).

“In PwrAgnt we have pinned/favorite projects at the top of our leftmost sidebar, always. On PwrGit we have faves shown on faves lens only. On regular lens they don’t appear at the top of the leftmost project sidebar, which is annoying. … on the all projects tab we do not even sort the pins to the top? … Make the UX as consistent as possible with PwrAgent.”

“I’m not even positive we need the pins lens anymore after this… but we can leave it for now.”

1

A pin is the repository’s own star

Only repo.pinned lifts a repository above the seam. A pinned worktree or branch still admits its repository to the Pinned lens, but below the seam (its star is not lit).

2

One order, every lens, both columns

The hand order of the pins is the same list in Focused, Pinned, Behind, Stale and All, in the index and in the tree. A lens only decides which pins are listed and how the rest is ordered.

3

The Pinned lens stays

Unchanged in what it lists. It no longer is the only place an order can be made.

?

Open: grouped by folder

Turn 4. Recommended: a Pinned group above the folder groups, so “always at the top” holds there too.

?

Open: Focused no longer leads with the current repository

An unpinned current repository now sits under the pins, first of the rest. Its row keeps the selection tint and the “Current” reason.
