TURN 1

One frame from open to close

The user: "the height of the dialog keeps changing as it goes through the different screens… very distracting." .modal.maintenance already had a fixed height: min(720px, 82vh), but four later rules set it back to height: auto whenever the dialog held the plan, the Combined step rail, the results receipt or the Worktrees panel. The top edge stays put (the backdrop hangs the dialog 12vh down), so the bottom edge and the footer moved instead. Measured in the harness at 1000×650: 569 → 507 → 560 → 507 → 569px as you step through the tabs and back. At 1100×900: 790 → 712 → 560 → 712 → 790. Proposal, as in PwrAgnt's Thread maintenance (#3456): every screen draws at the same height. The head, tabs, step rail and footer keep their size, and only the body scrolls.

TURN 2

The primary action is last and orange, and at the end it is Close

The user: "at the end the Close is not the rightmost button and is not a CTA color. Seems kinda odd and hard to find." Rule for every footer: the screen's primary action is the last button and uses the CTA style (modal__create). Secondary actions go to its left, and none follows a primary. When the job is done, the primary action is Close. Running it again becomes a secondary action and keeps its label.
