Every state is drawn per button, so the chevron lights up as a separate little box, and the menu’s open state barely registers. 1

A three-sided orange box

Hover paints only the chevron’s border, and it has no left edge, so the orange stops at a grey seam that belongs to the face (b, c). That is the box in the operator’s screenshot.

2

Open looks like hover

--bg-hover-strong on a 20px strip is nearly invisible beside the orange border, and the caret still points down, so nothing says “this is what opened the menu” (c).

3

Two focus rings for one control

Each half draws its own outline, so focus jumps between two differently sized boxes as you tab (d).

4

Busy tints only the face’s box

The chevron keeps its grey border beside the orange face, so busy reads as half a control (e).

✓

What PwrAgnt does

One pill owns the border and radius, and clips both zones (overflow: hidden). A hairline seam shows only at rest. Hover fills the zone under the pointer. Open fills the caret zone and flips the caret. One focus ring around the pill, and the inner buttons’ outlines are off.

2Proposed: one bordered control with two zones

PwrAgnt’s pill, in the toolbar’s own register: same 30px height, 7px radius and quiet fills as Add and the wrench, not the composer’s solid orange (Send is the composer’s one primary action; this is one of three toolbar buttons). .sidebar-toolbar__split owns the border; the face and chevron shed theirs.

1

The border belongs to the pair

Hover anywhere lights the whole outline in --accent-border, like every other toolbar button; the zone under the pointer fills with --bg-hover-strong, so it is still obvious which half a click fires.

2

The seam fades once a zone fills

The fill’s own edge is the boundary; a hairline beside it would read as a stray sliver (PwrAgnt’s note, same reason).

3

Open is its own state

Chevron zone on --accent-soft with an --accent-bright caret that points up (ChevronGlyph up, drawn, not rotated, per lib), while the outline stays lit. It holds after the pointer leaves, since the menu now stays open.

4

One focus ring

:has(:focus-visible) rings the split; the halves’ own outlines are off, or the clip would leave an orange bar at the seam (PwrAgnt hit exactly that).

5

Busy stays in the shared tint list

The face keeps its [aria-busy] fill; the split takes the accent border through :has(> [aria-busy]), so the whole control reads busy.
